# Government Schemes for Indian Farmers — Complete Guide

## 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Benefit**: Rs. 6,000/year in 3 instalments of Rs. 2,000 each
- **Eligibility**: All farmer families with cultivable land
- **Exclusion**: Income tax payers, institutional landholders, government employees
- **How to apply**: Visit pmkisan.gov.in or nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
- **Documents needed**: Aadhaar card, bank account, land records
- **Helpline**: 155261 or 011-24300606

## 2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Purpose**: Crop insurance against natural calamities, pests, and diseases
- **Premium**: Kharif: 2% of sum insured, Rabi: 1.5%, Horticultural: 5%
- **Coverage**: Prevented sowing, standing crop losses, post-harvest losses (14 days), localized calamities
- **How to apply**: Through bank (if loanee farmer) or CSC portal pmfby.gov.in
- **Claim process**: Report crop loss within 72 hours to insurance company or toll-free 14447
- **Season**: Enrol before cut-off date (varies by state)
- **Helpline**: 1800-180-1551

## 3. Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- **Purpose**: Short-term credit for crop production, post-harvest, and consumption
- **Credit limit**: Based on land holding and crop pattern (up to Rs. 3 lakh at 7% interest)
- **Interest subvention**: Additional 3% for timely repayment (effective rate: 4%)
- **Coverage**: Crop loans, term loans, consumption expenses
- **How to apply**: Visit nearest bank branch with land records, Aadhaar, and photo
- **Additional benefit**: Personal accident insurance of Rs. 50,000 (death) and Rs. 25,000 (disability)
- **Repayment**: Within 12 months for short-term crop loans

## 4. Soil Health Card Scheme
- **Purpose**: Provide soil testing results and crop-specific fertilizer recommendations
- **Benefit**: Free soil testing every 2 years
- **Parameters tested**: N, P, K, pH, EC, organic carbon, micro-nutrients (S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B)
- **How to apply**: Contact nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agriculture department
- **Website**: soilhealth.dac.gov.in

## 5. PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (Pension Scheme)
- **Purpose**: Pension for small and marginal farmers (land up to 2 hectares)
- **Benefit**: Rs. 3,000/month pension after age 60
- **Contribution**: Rs. 55 to Rs. 200/month (based on entry age 18-40 years)
- **Government contribution**: Equal matching contribution
- **How to apply**: Visit nearest CSC with Aadhaar and bank details

## 6. e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
- **Purpose**: Online trading platform connecting APMCs across India
- **Benefit**: Transparent price discovery, reduced intermediaries, better prices
- **How to use**: Register at enam.gov.in, link Aadhaar, trade through local APMC
- **Current status**: 1,361 mandis in 23 states connected
- **Features**: Real-time price information, quality testing, online payment

## 7. Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)
- **Purpose**: Promote organic farming
- **Benefit**: Rs. 50,000/hectare over 3 years (Rs. 31,000 for organic inputs)
- **Cluster approach**: Group of 50+ farmers, minimum 20 hectares
- **Certification**: Free PGS (Participatory Guarantee System) organic certification
- **How to apply**: Through state agriculture department or KVK

## 8. Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)
- **Purpose**: Subsidized farm machinery
- **Subsidy**: 40-50% for general category, 50-80% for SC/ST/women farmers
- **Equipment covered**: Tractors, rotavators, seed drills, sprayers, combine harvesters
- **How to apply**: Through agrimachinery.nic.in portal
- **Custom Hiring Centres**: Rent machinery if you can't afford to buy

## 9. Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY)
- **Purpose**: Overall agricultural development
- **Components**: Infrastructure, value addition, market access
- **How to benefit**: Through state agriculture department schemes

## 10. National Mission on Micro Irrigation (NMMI/PMKSY)
- **Purpose**: Promote drip and sprinkler irrigation
- **Subsidy**: 55% for small/marginal farmers, 45% for others
- **Benefit**: Save 30-50% water, increase yield by 20-40%
- **How to apply**: Through state horticulture department
- **Components**: Per Drop More Crop, Har Khet Ko Pani

## Important Helplines
- Kisan Call Centre: 1800-180-1551 (toll-free, all agriculture queries)
- PM-KISAN Helpline: 155261
- PMFBY Helpline: 14447
- eNAM Helpline: 1800-270-0224
- KVK Portal: kvk.icar.gov.in (find your nearest KVK)
