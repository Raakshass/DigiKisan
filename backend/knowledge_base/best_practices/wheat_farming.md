# Wheat (Gehun) — Complete Farming Guide for Indian Farmers

## Overview
Wheat is India's second most important cereal crop after rice. Major wheat-growing states include Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana, and Rajasthan. India produces approximately 110 million tonnes of wheat annually.

## Varieties Recommended by ICAR
- **HD-3226 (Pusa Yashasvi)**: High yield, rust resistant, suitable for irrigated timely sown conditions in North Western Plains Zone (NWPZ).
- **HD-2967**: Most popular variety in India, suitable for late sown conditions.
- **PBW-725**: High yield potential, suitable for Punjab, Haryana, and western UP.
- **WH-1105**: Good for Haryana and nearby areas, resistant to yellow rust.
- **DBW-187 (Karan Vandana)**: Suitable for timely sown irrigated conditions in NWPZ.
- **K-1006**: Suitable for eastern UP and Bihar.

## Sowing Season
- **Timely sowing**: October 25 to November 25 (best yields)
- **Late sowing**: November 25 to December 25 (reduced yields, use late-sown varieties)
- **Very late sowing**: After December 25 (significant yield loss, not recommended)

## Seed Rate
- Timely sown: 100 kg/hectare
- Late sown: 125 kg/hectare
- Seed treatment: Treat seeds with Carbendazim (2.5g/kg seed) or Thiram (2.5g/kg seed) before sowing

## Soil and Land Preparation
- Best suited: Well-drained loamy to clay-loam soils with pH 6.0-7.5
- Plough 2-3 times with disc plough followed by planking
- Apply 5 tonnes FYM/compost per hectare during land preparation
- Ensure fine tilth for proper germination

## Irrigation Schedule
- **First irrigation (CRI stage)**: 21-25 days after sowing — MOST CRITICAL
- **Second irrigation (Tillering)**: 40-45 days after sowing
- **Third irrigation (Jointing)**: 60-65 days after sowing
- **Fourth irrigation (Flowering)**: 80-85 days after sowing
- **Fifth irrigation (Dough stage)**: 100-105 days after sowing
- If water is limited, prioritize CRI and flowering stage irrigations.

## Fertilizer Recommendation (per hectare)
- Nitrogen (N): 120-150 kg (half at sowing, half at first irrigation)
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg (all at sowing)
- Potassium (K2O): 40 kg (all at sowing)
- Zinc Sulphate: 25 kg/hectare (if deficient)

## Major Diseases
1. **Yellow/Stripe Rust** (Puccinia striiformis): Yellow-orange pustules on leaves. Spray Propiconazole 25EC (0.1%) at first appearance.
2. **Brown/Leaf Rust** (Puccinia recondita): Brown pustules on upper leaf surface. Spray Mancozeb 75WP (0.25%).
3. **Karnal Bunt** (Tilletia indica): Black powdery mass in grains. Seed treatment with Carboxin + Thiram.
4. **Powdery Mildew**: White powdery growth on leaves. Spray Sulphur 80WP (0.3%).
5. **Loose Smut**: Entire ear replaced by black spore mass. Seed treatment with Carboxin.

## Major Pests
1. **Aphids**: Suck sap from leaves and ears. Spray Dimethoate 30EC (1ml/litre) or Imidacloprid 17.8SL (0.3ml/litre).
2. **Termites**: Damage roots and lower stems. Apply Chlorpyriphos 20EC in soil.
3. **Pink stem borer**: Bore into stems. Apply Carbofuran 3G granules.

## Harvesting
- Harvest when grain moisture is 20-25% and grain becomes hard
- Usually ready in 120-150 days after sowing (March-April)
- Use combine harvester or manual harvesting with sickle
- Dry grain to 12% moisture before storage

## MSP (Minimum Support Price) 2024-25
- Wheat MSP: Rs. 2,275/quintal
- Contact nearest APMC mandi or FCI procurement centre for selling at MSP

## Government Schemes
- **PM-KISAN**: Rs. 6,000/year direct benefit transfer to farmers
- **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)**: Crop insurance at 1.5% premium for Rabi crops
- **Soil Health Card Scheme**: Free soil testing and fertilizer recommendations
