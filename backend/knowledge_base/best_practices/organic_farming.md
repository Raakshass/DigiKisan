# Organic Farming Practices for Indian Farmers

## What is Organic Farming?
Organic farming is a production system that avoids synthetic fertilizers, pesticides, growth regulators, and GMOs. It relies on crop rotation, compost, biological pest control, and natural inputs to maintain soil fertility and ecological balance.

## Why Switch to Organic?
- **Higher market price**: Organic produce fetches 20-40% premium
- **Lower input cost**: No expensive chemical inputs after transition
- **Soil health improvement**: Better water retention, microbial activity
- **Health benefits**: No pesticide residues in food
- **Export potential**: Growing global demand for organic Indian products
- **Government support**: PKVY scheme provides Rs. 50,000/ha over 3 years

## Organic Manures and Fertilizers

### 1. Vermicompost
- **What**: Worm-processed organic waste
- **How to make**: Dig a pit (3m x 1m x 1m), layer cow dung + crop waste + earthworms (Eisenia foetida), keep moist, ready in 60-90 days
- **Application**: 5-10 tonnes/hectare
- **Benefits**: Rich in NPK, beneficial microbes, improves soil structure

### 2. Jeevamrut (Liquid Biofertilizer)
- **Recipe**: 200L water + 10kg fresh cow dung + 10L cow urine + 2kg jaggery + 2kg pulse flour + handful of forest soil
- **Preparation**: Mix and ferment for 5-7 days, stir daily
- **Application**: Dilute and apply through irrigation or foliar spray every 15 days
- **Benefits**: Multiplies beneficial soil microorganisms

### 3. Panchagavya
- **Ingredients**: Cow dung, cow urine, milk, curd, ghee (all from desi cow)
- **Recipe**: Mix cow dung (7kg) + cow urine (3L), ferment 3 days. Add milk (2L) + curd (2L) + ghee (1kg), ferment 15 days
- **Application**: 3% solution as foliar spray; 5% through irrigation
- **Benefits**: Growth promoter, pest repellent, improves fruit quality

### 4. Beejamrut (Seed Treatment)
- **Recipe**: 5L water + 5kg fresh cow dung + 5L cow urine + 50g lime + handful of forest soil
- **How to use**: Soak seeds for 20 minutes before sowing, dry in shade
- **Benefits**: Protects seeds from soil-borne diseases, promotes germination

## Organic Pest Management

### Neem-based Sprays
- **Neem Kernel Extract**: 5% solution (50g neem kernel powder/litre water), soak overnight, filter, spray
- **Neem Oil**: 2-3ml/litre with soap solution as emulsifier
- **Effective against**: Aphids, whiteflies, caterpillars, leaf miners, mealybugs

### Dashparni Ark (10-Leaf Extract)
- **Ingredients**: Neem, karanj, datura, aak, custard apple, tulsi, lantana, besharam, papaya leaves + cow urine + water
- **Preparation**: Crush 2kg each of 10 leaves, add 10L cow urine + 100L water, ferment 30 days
- **Application**: 250ml per 15L pump
- **Effective against**: Most sucking and chewing pests

### Trichoderma (Biological Fungicide)
- **Application**: Mix 2.5kg Trichoderma viride per hectare with FYM, apply to soil
- **Effective against**: Fusarium wilt, root rot, damping off, Pythium

## Crop Rotation for Organic Farming
- Year 1 (Kharif): Rice → (Rabi): Wheat
- Year 2 (Kharif): Maize/Millets → (Rabi): Mustard/Chickpea
- Year 3 (Kharif): Pulses (Moong/Urad) → (Rabi): Vegetable crops
- Always include one legume crop per rotation to fix nitrogen

## Organic Certification in India
- **PGS-India (Participatory Guarantee System)**: Group certification, free under PKVY
- **Third-party certification**: APEDA-accredited agencies (for export), costs Rs. 30,000-50,000
- **Transition period**: 2 years for annual crops, 3 years for perennial crops
- **Website**: pgsindia-ncof.gov.in

## Marketing Organic Produce
- **Jaivik Kheti Portal**: jaivikkheti.in — government's organic marketplace
- **Direct to consumer**: Farmers markets, subscription boxes
- **Organic stores**: BigBasket Organic, Organic Tattva, 24 Mantra
- **Export**: Through APEDA with organic certification (NPOP standard)
