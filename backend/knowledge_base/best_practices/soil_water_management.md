# Soil Health & Water Management Guide for Indian Farmers

## Soil Testing

### Why Test Soil?
- Know exactly what nutrients your soil needs — stop wasting money on unnecessary fertilizers
- Government provides FREE soil testing under Soil Health Card scheme
- Test every 2 years, or when switching crops

### How to Collect Soil Samples
1. Collect from 15-20 spots across the field in a zigzag pattern
2. Use khurpi or auger, dig 15cm (6 inches) deep for field crops, 30cm for orchards
3. Remove top surface debris before digging
4. Mix all sub-samples in a clean bucket
5. Take 500g of the mixed sample in a clean cloth bag
6. Label with: Name, Village, Field number, Crop to be grown, Date
7. Submit to nearest Soil Testing Lab or KVK
8. DO NOT collect near trees, bunds, manure heaps, or compost pits

### Understanding Soil Health Card Results

| Parameter | Low | Medium | High | Action if Low |
|-----------|-----|--------|------|---------------|
| pH | <6.0 (acidic) | 6.0-7.5 (ideal) | >8.0 (alkaline) | Lime for acidic, Gypsum for alkaline |
| Organic Carbon | <0.5% | 0.5-0.75% | >0.75% | Add FYM, compost, green manure |
| Nitrogen (N) | <280 kg/ha | 280-560 | >560 | Apply urea based on crop need |
| Phosphorus (P) | <10 kg/ha | 10-25 | >25 | Apply DAP or SSP |
| Potassium (K) | <120 kg/ha | 120-280 | >280 | Apply MOP (Muriate of Potash) |
| Zinc (Zn) | <0.6 ppm | 0.6-1.2 | >1.2 | Apply Zinc Sulphate 25 kg/ha |
| Sulphur (S) | <10 ppm | 10-20 | >20 | Apply Elemental Sulphur or Gypsum |

## Fertilizer Management

### Types of Fertilizers
- **Urea (46% N)**: Most common nitrogen fertilizer. Apply in splits, not all at once.
- **DAP (18% N + 46% P2O5)**: Apply at sowing. Do NOT use for top dressing.
- **MOP (60% K2O)**: Apply at sowing or in splits. Essential for fruits and root crops.
- **SSP (16% P + 11% S + 19% Ca)**: Better than DAP for sulphur-deficient soils.
- **NPK Complex (12-32-16, 10-26-26, etc.)**: Convenient but often imbalanced use.
- **Neem-coated Urea**: Government mandated. Reduces N losses by 10-15%.

### Fertilizer Application Best Practices
1. Always base fertilizer use on soil test results
2. Apply phosphorus and potassium at sowing (they don't move in soil)
3. Split nitrogen into 2-3 doses (basal + top dressing)
4. Never broadcast urea on dry soil — band place or incorporate
5. Use Neem-coated urea to slow nitrogen release
6. Apply micronutrients (Zn, Fe, B) based on deficiency symptoms

### Integrated Nutrient Management (INM)
- 50% nutrient from chemical fertilizers + 50% from organic sources
- Sources: FYM, vermicompost, green manure, biofertilizers
- Biofertilizers: Rhizobium (for pulses), Azotobacter (for cereals), PSB (for phosphorus)
- Apply biofertilizers as seed treatment or soil application

## Water Management

### Irrigation Methods Comparison

| Method | Water Saving | Cost | Best For |
|--------|-------------|------|----------|
| Flood (traditional) | Baseline | Low | Rice |
| Furrow | 20-30% | Low | Row crops (sugarcane, potato) |
| Sprinkler | 30-40% | Medium | Wheat, vegetables, groundnut |
| Drip | 50-60% | High | Fruits, vegetables, sugarcane |
| Micro-sprinkler | 40-50% | Medium-High | Orchards, spices |

### Government Subsidy on Micro-Irrigation (PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop)
- **Drip**: 55% subsidy for small/marginal farmers, 45% for others
- **Sprinkler**: 55% subsidy for small/marginal, 45% for others
- Apply through state horticulture department
- Companies: Jain Irrigation, Netafim, Finolex

### Rainwater Harvesting
- Farm pond: Minimum 20m x 20m x 3m depth, line with plastic sheet
- Rooftop harvesting: 1 inch rain on 100 sqm roof = 1000 litres water
- Check dam: Community-level water storage
- Percolation tank: Recharge groundwater

### Drought Management
1. Mulching: Reduces evaporation by 25-30%
2. Use drought-tolerant varieties (Sahbhagi Dhan for rice, HI-1544 for wheat)
3. Reduce plant population by 25% to match available water
4. Apply potassium — improves drought tolerance
5. Life-saving irrigation: Prioritize at flowering stage if water is very limited
6. Crop insurance: Enrol in PMFBY before sowing

### Waterlogging Management
1. Provide drainage channels at 10m intervals
2. Raise bed planting for wheat and vegetables
3. Grow flood-tolerant varieties (CR Dhan 801 for rice)
4. Apply extra nitrogen after waterlogging (nitrogen gets lost)
5. Spray 2% urea as foliar feed for quick recovery
