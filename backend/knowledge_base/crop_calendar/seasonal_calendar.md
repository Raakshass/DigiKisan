# Crop Calendar — Rabi, Kharif, and Zaid Seasons in India

## Season Overview

| Season | Duration | Monsoon | Major Crops |
|--------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Kharif | June - October | Southwest monsoon | Rice, Maize, Cotton, Soybean, Groundnut, Arhar, Moong, Urad |
| Rabi | October - March | Post-monsoon (irrigation) | Wheat, Mustard, Gram, Barley, Peas, Lentil, Potato, Onion |
| Zaid | March - June | Pre-monsoon | Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Moong, Sunflower |

## Detailed Month-by-Month Calendar

### January
- **Rabi crops**: Second/third irrigation for wheat and mustard
- **Potato**: Main crop ready for harvesting (90-day varieties)
- **Vegetable nursery**: Prepare tomato, brinjal, chilli nurseries for summer crop
- **Wheat**: Apply second dose of nitrogen if not done
- **Sugarcane**: Prepare land, start planting spring sugarcane

### February
- **Wheat**: Critical flowering/heading stage — ensure irrigation
- **Mustard**: Flowering complete, pod formation stage
- **Potato**: Late crop harvesting
- **Summer vegetables**: Transplant tomato, brinjal, chilli seedlings
- **Onion**: Rabi onion in active bulb formation
- **Mango**: Flowering — spray Planofix (10 ppm) for better fruit set

### March
- **Wheat**: Grain filling stage, last irrigation
- **Mustard**: Harvesting begins
- **Zaid crops**: Sow watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, moong
- **Sugarcane**: Complete spring planting by mid-March
- **Summer ploughing**: Start preparing fields for Kharif

### April
- **Wheat**: Harvesting (late March to mid April in north India)
- **Rabi harvest**: Gram, barley, lentil harvesting
- **Mango**: Fruit development, spray against mango hopper
- **Summer vegetables**: Active fruiting phase
- **Seed procurement**: Arrange certified seeds for Kharif season
- **Soil testing**: Best time to collect soil samples

### May
- **Summer ploughing**: Deep ploughing (reduces pests by 80%)
- **Green manuring**: Sow dhaincha/sunhemp for Kharif field preparation
- **Nursery preparation**: Rice nursery beds preparation
- **Mango**: Fruit maturation and harvest begins
- **Purchase inputs**: Buy fertilizers, pesticides, seeds for Kharif
- **Farm pond**: Clean and deepen farm ponds before monsoon

### June
- **Rice nursery**: Sow rice nursery (June 1-15 for transplanted; June 15-30 for DSR)
- **Kharif sowing**: Sow maize, soybean, arhar, urad, moong with first monsoon rains
- **Cotton**: Sowing starts (June 1-30)
- **Sugarcane**: First earthing up, apply nitrogen
- **Green manure incorporation**: Plough in green manure crop

### July
- **Rice transplanting**: Transplant 25-30 day old seedlings
- **Kharif crops**: First weeding and intercultural operations
- **Cotton**: Thinning and gap filling
- **Maize**: Tasseling — ensure adequate moisture
- **Soybean**: Weed management critical in first 30 days
- **Pest scouting**: Start weekly pest monitoring

### August
- **Rice**: Tillering — apply second dose of nitrogen
- **Kharif pulses**: Flowering and pod formation
- **Cotton**: Square and boll formation, watch for bollworm
- **Late Kharif onion**: Nursery sowing
- **Vegetable nursery**: Sow cauliflower, cabbage for Rabi season
- **Watch for floods**: Keep drainage channels clear

### September
- **Rice**: Panicle initiation — critical irrigation stage
- **Kharif harvest prep**: Moong, urad harvesting begins
- **Rabi planning**: Prepare seed and inputs for Rabi season
- **Potato early crop**: Planting begins (Sept 25 onwards)
- **Onion Kharif**: Harvesting begins
- **Soybean**: Pod filling, avoid water stress

### October
- **Rice**: Harvesting (late varieties in November)
- **Kharif harvest**: Soybean, maize, cotton picking begins
- **Rabi sowing**: Wheat (Oct 25 onwards), mustard, gram sowing
- **Potato main crop**: Planting (Oct 15 - Nov 15)
- **Onion Rabi**: Nursery sowing
- **Sugarcane**: Ratoon management

### November
- **Wheat**: Timely sowing deadline (Nov 25)
- **Rabi crops**: Mustard, gram, barley, peas sowing
- **Rice stubble**: Manage stubble (DO NOT BURN — use Happy Seeder)
- **Onion**: Transplanting for Rabi crop
- **Potato**: Complete planting, first irrigation
- **Cotton**: Final picking

### December
- **Wheat**: Late sowing (use late varieties like HD-2985, WH-1124)
- **Rabi crops**: First irrigation for wheat (CRI stage at 21-25 DAS)
- **Potato**: Earthing up, second irrigation
- **Vegetables**: Harvest cauliflower, cabbage, peas
- **Year planning**: Review year's performance, plan next year
- **Soil testing**: Send samples for analysis

## State-Specific Notes

### Uttar Pradesh
- Largest agricultural state, grows nearly every major crop
- Sugarcane belt: Western UP (Meerut, Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar)
- Potato belt: Agra, Kanpur, Farrukhabad
- Wheat-rice is dominant rotation — government promoting crop diversification

### Punjab/Haryana
- Rice-wheat rotation dominant — groundwater crisis due to rice
- Government promoting DSR and maize/millets as alternatives
- Paddy sowing after June 15 (government rule to conserve groundwater)
- Happy Seeder mandatory for stubble management

### Madhya Pradesh
- Soybean capital of India
- Major wheat and gram producer
- Onion and garlic in Mandsaur-Neemuch belt
- Expanding organic farming under PKVY

### Maharashtra
- Onion: Nasik, Ahmednagar, Pune, Solapur
- Sugarcane: Western Maharashtra
- Cotton: Vidarbha region (rainfed, high farmer distress)
- Horticulture: Mango (Ratnagiri), Grapes (Nasik), Orange (Nagpur)

### Karnataka
- Coffee and spice plantations: Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru
- Ragi/Finger millet: Traditional crop, gaining premium market
- Silk production: Mysuru, Ramanagara
- Irrigation: Cauvery basin, Krishna basin
